Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about QuickBooks professional services.

What are professional services in QuickBooks?

QuickBooks professional services refer to expert setup, customization, training, troubleshooting, and ongoing support provided by certified QuickBooks ProAdvisors. These services help businesses streamline their accounting, improve accuracy, and maximize the use of QuickBooks features.

What are QuickBooks professionals called?

QuickBooks professionals are called ProAdvisors. They are certified accounting or bookkeeping experts who specialize in QuickBooks products and can provide consulting, setup, and ongoing support to businesses.

Do you need to be an accountant to be a QuickBooks ProAdvisor?

No, you don't need to be an accountant to become a QuickBooks ProAdvisor. Anyone can earn the certification by completing Intuit's training and passing the exam. However, many ProAdvisors are accountants, bookkeepers, or small business consultants.

How much does QuickBooks ProAdvisor certification cost?

The QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor Certification is free with a QuickBooks Online Accountant account. However, additional advanced certifications or training resources may have separate fees.

What's the difference between professional services and managed services?

Professional services are project-based solutions such as QuickBooks setup, data migration, or training. Managed services offer ongoing support and monitoring, including bookkeeping, payroll processing, and monthly QuickBooks maintenance.
