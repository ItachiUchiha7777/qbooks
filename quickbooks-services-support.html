Answers to common questions about our QuickBooks services.

What are QuickBooks professional services?

QuickBooks professional services include expert setup, customization, training, troubleshooting, and ongoing support provided by certified ProAdvisors. These services help businesses streamline accounting, improve accuracy, and maximize the value of QuickBooks.

Who are QuickBooks professionals?

QuickBooks professionals are certified ProAdvisors with expertise in QuickBooks products. They provide consulting, setup, training, and support to businesses of all sizes.

Do I need to be an accountant to become a ProAdvisor?

No, an accounting background is not required. Anyone can earn ProAdvisor certification by completing Intuit's training and passing the exam. Many ProAdvisors come from diverse professional backgrounds.

How much does ProAdvisor certification cost?

The QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor Certification is free with a QuickBooks Online Accountant account. Additional advanced certifications may have separate fees.

What's the difference between professional and managed services?

Professional services are project-based — like setup, migration, or training. Managed services provide ongoing support including bookkeeping, payroll, and regular system maintenance.
